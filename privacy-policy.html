Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AEC Revit Lab ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our Revit plugins and website at aecrevitlab.com.

1. Data We Collect

Our Revit plugins operate entirely offline and locally on your machine. They do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, project data, or usage information externally.

The only data stored by our plugins is your personal plugin settings and presets, saved locally to your machine at:

  • %APPDATA%\AECRevitLab\DrawingRegisterExporter\

This data never leaves your machine and is never accessible to us.

On our website (aecrevitlab.com), we may collect:

  • Email addresses voluntarily submitted via our "Notify me" form for product launch notifications
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) via our hosting provider

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Email addresses collected via our notification form are used solely to notify you when a requested product launches
  • We do not use your data for advertising or share it with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Web server logs are used only for security and performance monitoring

3. Third Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (github.com/privacy)
  • Formspree — email form submissions (formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing the data they process. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

4. Data Retention

  • Email addresses submitted for launch notifications are retained until the product launches or you request deletion
  • Local plugin settings on your machine are retained until you manually delete them or uninstall the plugin

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your email address from our notification list at any time
  • Delete your local plugin settings at any time by removing the folder at %APPDATA%\AECRevitLab\

To request deletion of your data, contact us at admin@aecrevitlab.com.

6. Data Security

Our plugins do not transmit any data externally. Email addresses submitted via our website are processed by Formspree under their own security standards. We do not store payment information — all purchases are handled by Autodesk's App Store platform.

7. Children's Privacy

Our plugins and website are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
